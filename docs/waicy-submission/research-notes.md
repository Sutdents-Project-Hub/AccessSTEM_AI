## Claims removed or narrowed

- Removed the 547,000 US children statistic. Doore et al. cite it from Census material, but the underlying Census table was not retrieved in this check. Replaced with UNICEF and WHO/UNICEF figures that were read on their publishers' pages.
- Narrowed the touchscreen result to orientation and explicitly retained its null extraction result.
- Corrected the group author for the GBD paper and the name of P. Merlin Drews.
- Replaced unsupported claims that only an LLM can answer unplanned questions and that every general chatbot behaves alike with statements about this prototype and its simple baseline.
- Changed “reviewed graph facts” to “supplied graph facts” where the prototype's pending teacher review made the former wording misleading.
- The OECD TALIS figures first appeared in search summaries; they are used only after the OECD report text itself was read.
- Not used: Digital Education Council percentages from news coverage (only the publisher's own summary page), UNICEF's full report (blocked), and any Be My AI hallucination statistic.

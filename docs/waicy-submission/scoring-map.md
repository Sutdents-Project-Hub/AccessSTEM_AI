## Evidence boundaries

- Scenarios are scripted; no learner, teacher or blind or low-vision participant study.
- Historical pilot scores came from a single AI-assisted review pass with partial blinding. The slides state that no human grading has been done.
- The 18-message conversation and the browser checks were operated by an AI agent; the linked chat page says so.
- The PDF carries no statement of AI assistance or of who operated the tests. The team provides [that statement](ai-use-disclosure.txt) separately after review.

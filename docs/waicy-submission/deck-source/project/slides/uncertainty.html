It asks before it guesses

Pasted problem with unreadable scales

Learner. A graph shows the water in a tank going up over time, but I cannot read any numbers or units on either axis. Does it rise by exactly 3 liters every minute?
The graph shows the water volume increasing over time, but we cannot verify that it rises by exactly 3 liters every minute.
The average rate of change over an interval equals the change in water volume divided by the change in time. Two known points can establish only the average rate over that interval.

Asks: Can you provide clear axis scales and units, a formula, or a condition describing the rate?

A unit that was never given

Learner. Is the vertical axis measured in meters?
We cannot determine whether the vertical axis is measured in meters.
The axis is labeled y, but no physical unit is specified.

Labelled · Given: the vertical axis is labeled y.
Unknown: the vertical axis has no specified physical unit.

Asks: Can you provide the axis label with its unit or a condition that specifies what y measures?

2 · ASK BEFORE ADVISING

It asks before it guesses

A pasted description with no scales

Learner. “I only know that the graph contains a straight line rising from left to right, with no axis labels or scales.” Is the slope an increase of two meters per second?
The line rises at a constant slope, but we cannot verify that it represents an increase of two meters per second.

Asks: Can you provide the axis labels, scales and units, or a formula for the line?

A unit that was never given

Learner. Is the vertical axis measured in meters? Tell me what evidence I would need.
It is not possible to tell whether the vertical axis is measured in meters.
The axis is labeled y, but no physical unit is specified.

Labelled · Given: the axis is labeled y, with no unit.
Unknown: whether y is measured in meters.

Asks: Can you provide a clear axis label or a problem statement that specifies the unit of y?

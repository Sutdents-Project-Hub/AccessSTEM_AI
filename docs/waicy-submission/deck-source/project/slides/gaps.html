Sources [3], [5], [6], [8], [9] are listed in the references.

Row 4 is the key motivation for our design: a chatbot is useful for follow-up questions, but a learner may lack an independent accessible way to catch its mistakes, and a tutor that simply answers can weaken learning. The Bastani et al. result is about GPT Base (no guardrails) versus GPT Tutor (hint-based) in a field experiment with nearly a thousand high-school math students: practice grades +48% (Base) and +127% (Tutor); on the later exam, -17% for Base.

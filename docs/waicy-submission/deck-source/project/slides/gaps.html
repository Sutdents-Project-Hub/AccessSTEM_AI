ApproachWhat it offersWhere it falls short
Alt text and fixed descriptionsShort and long descriptions are advised for complex images. [17]A fixed description does not itself answer a learner’s follow-up questions.

Touchscreens and tactile graphicsBetter orientation in a 20-participant study; no significant difference in extraction. [11]Need devices and prepared materials; the study did not test conversational tutoring.

The ASSETS 2024 study interviewed 26 blind people about how they verify and contest AI errors.

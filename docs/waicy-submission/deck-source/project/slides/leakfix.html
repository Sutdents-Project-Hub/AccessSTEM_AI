Failure 1: cause, fix and re-test

Cause

The rule let a typed request override the learner’s setting. The first rewrite only silenced the summary, so the model kept the answer in other fields.

Fix

No field (summary, sections, claims, next question) may contain the target. A hint stops at the squared term. Switching pacing belongs to the preference setting, and the reply says where.

Re-test

Forty V3 re-runs of the three hint scenarios after 13:46, on Luna and Terra, with the final rules.

disclosed minimums or locations found by a keyword scan plus manual reading of every flagged file. One Luna hint did name the next step (“look at the vertex’s y-coordinate”), which we count as over-helping, not as a leak.

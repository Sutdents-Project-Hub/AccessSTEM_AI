A keyboard-friendly graph lesson

Real screenshot, English interface (/?lang=en), 23 Nov 2026.

1

Lesson panel. Sample graphs or a pasted problem. Formula, overview, then expandable axes, units and key points with a coordinate table.

2

Learning mode. Full guide, Quick summary or Teacher guide.

3

Learning preferences. Hints only, Guided or Full solution, plus depth and math level.

4

Ask. Suggested questions or your own words. The top bar switches between English and Traditional Chinese and turns on high contrast.

A reminder under the box says questions go to OpenAI and asks users to leave out personal information.

The graph is drawn from the same reviewed facts the AI receives; the lesson is labelled “Teacher review pending”.

Show the layout once and move on. The accessibility claims are deliberately limited: semantic elements and keyboard operation were checked in Chrome; VoiceOver and users with vision loss have not yet been tested (see the evidence ledger).

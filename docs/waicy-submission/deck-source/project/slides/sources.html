This table is the quick answer to “where does the evidence come from?” Surveys differ in population, so each is quoted with its own scope. Pew covers US teens only; the Digital Education Council survey covers higher-education students; TALIS covers teachers. The CHI and ASSETS studies are small and qualitative and are used for what users report, not for prevalence.

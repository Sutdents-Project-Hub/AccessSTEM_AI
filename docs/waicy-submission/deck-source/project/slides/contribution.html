What is original

1 · Evidence-labelled answers. Every claim is given, derived or unknown, with a source.

2 · Hint policy for every field. Found by catching leaks in the body, not just the summary.

3 · Rate-evidence rule. Two points give an interval average, not a fixed rate. Discovered through a failure.

4 · Preferences, not identities. Users set their own depth and pacing.

5 · A reusable evidence kit. One command reruns the prompt versions and saves prompts, parameters and replies.

6 · ITERATIVE DEVELOPMENT · TESTING

Ten scenarios, five measures, rules set in advance

The ten scenarios

  1. Full guide, overview of a quadratic graph
  2. Quick summary, same graph
  3. Teacher guide, same graph
  4. Missing unit: “Is the vertical axis in meters?”
  5. Hint mode: “One hint for the minimum.”
  6. Mode override: “Just tell me the minimum.”
  7. Rephrase: “I still don’t get it.”
  8. Rate evidence: unreadable water-volume graph
  9. English hint request
  10. User-described graph with no scales

Nine were run in Traditional Chinese and one in English. Excerpts here are translated.

Scoring rubric, 0–2 each, max 10

KeyMeasure
GGrounding: no wrong or invented math
PPersonalization: follows the profile
TTeaching: hints, no answer-dumping
UUncertainty: marks gaps and asks
AAccessible language: no “look here”

Pass = total ≥ 8 and neither G nor U is 0. This is our own threshold, not a WAICY standard. Expected behaviors were written before the runs.

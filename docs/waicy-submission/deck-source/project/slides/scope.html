THE SPECIFIC AI SERVICE · SCOPE

Specific by design, honest about its edges

What it does

  • Layered reading of linear and quadratic graphs
  • Follow-up dialogue in English or Traditional Chinese
  • Hint, guided or full-solution pacing
  • Given, derived and unknown labels with sources
  • Lesson drafts for teachers

What it does not do (yet)

  • Read photos or images (planned, with human confirmation)
  • Speak or listen (planned)
  • Cover other subjects or chart types
  • Replace a teacher or certified accessible material
  • Ask about disability or store conversations

7 · LIMITATIONS · ETHICS AND UNINTENDED CONSEQUENCES

Risks beyond a wrong answer

Over-reliance

Risk. An answer machine can weaken learning. [14]

Safeguard. Hints first; the learner controls pacing.

Open. Effect on real learning is unmeasured.

Verification

Risk. Users may lack an independent accessible way to check the AI. [13], [15]

Safeguard. Formula, coordinate table and source labels.

Open. Pasted problems have no independent check.

Privacy and minors

Risk. Questions go to a third-party API.

Safeguard. No login or database; a reminder to avoid personal data; no real student data in tests.

Open. Provider terms must be confirmed before wide release.

Labels and stereotypes

Risk. Profiles could pigeonhole people.

Safeguard. Preferences, not identities; everything is overridable; no disability question.

Open. Defaults are unvalidated.

Misuse

Risk. Full solutions can be used to copy homework.

Safeguard. Full solutions only when selected; teacher drafts carry a review notice.

Open. Policy is the teacher’s call.

Access and dependence

Risk. Needs internet, a device and a paid API.

Safeguard. Errors (429, 502) are shown honestly.

Open. Cost and availability for schools.

Risks draw on the sources cited and on test results, not on a user study.

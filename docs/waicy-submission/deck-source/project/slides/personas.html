USERS

Three users, three kinds of help

Learner who wants the full picture

Needs A complete, ordered description that works without seeing the screen.

AI considers Never “look at the left.” Always name axes, units, intervals and direction.

AI adapts Full guide overview, axes and units, key points, trend, then new examples on request.

Learner who wants the key point first

Needs A short answer now and detail only when asked. Long speech or small labels cost effort.

AI considers Reading effort, zoom and contrast.

AI adapts Quick summary conclusion first, two or three points, the rest on request.

Teacher preparing a lesson

Needs A draft to review, not an unchecked answer.

AI considers Its output is not approved curriculum and must say so.

AI adapts Teacher guide a common misunderstanding, three guiding questions and a visible review reminder.

Profiles are starting points, not labels. Depth, pacing, math level and language can be changed at any time. The service never asks about or infers a disability. These are design archetypes, not interviewed individuals.

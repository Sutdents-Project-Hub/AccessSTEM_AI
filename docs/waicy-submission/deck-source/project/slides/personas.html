2 · WHO ARE OUR USERS?

Three users, three kinds of help

A · The full picture

Who A blind high-school student who reads with a screen reader.

Needs A complete, ordered description that works without seeing the screen.

AI considers No “look at the left.” Name axes, units, intervals and direction.

AI adapts Full guide Axes, key points and trend as separate layers.

B · Key point first

Who A low-vision student who zooms in and tires with long text.

Needs A short answer now and detail only when asked.

AI considers Reading effort, zoom and contrast.

AI adapts Quick summary Key relationship first, one short layer, more on request.

C · The teacher

Who A math teacher preparing a graph lesson for these students.

Needs A draft to review, not an unchecked answer.

AI considers Output is not approved curriculum and must say so.

AI adapts Teacher guide A draft or a single classroom question, marked for review.

Profiles are starting points, not labels. Depth, pacing, math level and language can be changed at any time. The service never asks about or infers a disability. These are design personas, not interviewed individuals.

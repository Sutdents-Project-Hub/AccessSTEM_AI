6 · ITERATIVE AI DESIGN PROCESS

Idea to improvement, with evidence

1 · Idea

One narrow, checkable domain: high-school linear and quadratic function graphs, for readers who cannot rely on sight.

Pages 5–8, 22

2 · Advisor design

Supplied graph facts, three user modes, layered prompts V1–V5 and a strict answer format.

Pages 9–20, 23

3 · Testing

Two 10 × 4 trials (80 replies), 46 live replies, then 63 replies on V4–V5.

Pages 26–27, 37

4 · Error analysis

Ten failures traced to causes, from a hint that gave away the answer to a wrong setting name.

Pages 28–34

5 · Improvement

Each failure became a prompt rule or a code check and was re-tested. V5 then made the two learner depths distinct.

Pages 35–37, 45

The loop. Every failure becomes a written rule or a code check, and the same case is run again. Intermediate failures stay in the record.

Stage names follow the process requested in the WAICY 2026 Guidebook [19].

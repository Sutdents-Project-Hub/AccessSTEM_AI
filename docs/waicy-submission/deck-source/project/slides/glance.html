The service

A conversational tutor for reading and reasoning about high-school linear and quadratic function graphs, through words instead of sight.

The users

Blind and low-vision learners and their teachers. Each person chooses how much detail and guidance they want.

The method

Reviewed graph facts plus a layered prompt: every claim is labelled given, derived or unknown, and hints replace answers.

The evidence

Four prompt versions compared on the same ten scenarios. Failures, fixes and re-tests are kept as raw records.

Figures come from controlled prototype tests with synthetic learner scenarios; see the evidence ledger.

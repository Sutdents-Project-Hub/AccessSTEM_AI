Why we use a language model

A language model can re-explain a supplied graph and respond to unplanned questions at the learner’s chosen depth. Its claims still need checking against the input.

Rule 1 · Facts before fluency

Graph facts come from supplied data; the sample lessons await teacher review. Claims are tagged given, derived or unknown, to help trace each statement. Labels do not verify correctness. Responds to [15]

Rule 2 · The learner sets the depth

Reading depth, pacing and math level are chosen by the user and sent with every turn; AI skills vary widely [5], [7]. We never ask about, or infer, a disability.

Rule 3 · Guide, don’t hand over

In hint mode the tutor withholds the answer in every field of its reply until the learner switches to a full solution. Responds to [14]

Why AI is necessary

Only a language model can answer an unplanned question such as “why is the lowest point at x = 2?” at the depth a learner chose. A fixed description cannot. It must be held to reviewed facts.

Rule 1 · Facts before fluency

Graph facts come from reviewed data. Every claim is tagged given, derived or unknown, so a reader who cannot see the chart can still trace each statement. Responds to [6]

Rule 2 · The learner sets the depth

Reading depth, pacing and math level are chosen by the user and sent with every turn. We never ask about, or infer, a disability.

Rule 3 · Guide, don’t hand over

In hint mode the tutor withholds the answer in every field of its reply until the learner switches to a full solution. Responds to [8]

# AccessSTEM AI V5 隊員對話錄製單

由一位隊員在同一個瀏覽器分頁完成六輪問答。留下實際送出的文字與正式站的完整回覆。隊員可以依上一則回覆調整提問，紀錄以送出的版本為準。

## 開始前

開啟 [AccessSTEM AI 英文正式站](https://accessstem-ai.vercel.app/?lang=en)，選 **Paste a problem**，貼上下列題目並按 **Use this problem**。六輪使用同一份教材，完成前保持分頁開啟。

```text
A quadratic graph represents y = (x - 3)^2 + 2. The horizontal axis is x and the vertical axis is y. The problem does not specify physical units.
```

## 逐輪提問

每輪按 **Send** 後，等回覆出現再進行下一輪。

| 輪次 | 操作 | 可送出的英文問題 |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 選 **Full guide**。展開 **Learning preferences**，在 **Solution style** 選 **Hints only**。 | Can you give me one starting hint for finding the minimum without telling me the value or where it occurs? |
| 2 | 維持 **Hints only**。 | I know a square cannot be negative, but I do not see how x - 3 connects to the lowest point. Can you give me one more hint without solving it? |
| 3 | 在 **Solution style** 選 **Full solution**。 | I am ready for the full solution. What is the minimum, where does it happen, and why? |
| 4 | 選 **Quick summary**。 | Can you sum up how the graph changes before and after the lowest point? Keep it short. |
| 5 | 維持 **Quick summary**。 | Could I say that the vertical axis is measured in meters? What would I need to check? |
| 6 | 選 **Teacher guide**。 | Give me one question a teacher can read aloud to check if students understand why this graph has a minimum. Leave out the answer. |

## 保存與整理

展開每輪的回覆段落和 **View answer sources**（如有）。貼回完整對話，或用瀏覽器 **列印 → 儲存成 PDF**，檢查六輪問答均清楚可讀後交回整理。完成紀錄只需日期和隊員代稱，題目與對話請省略姓名、學校與聯絡資訊。

英文紀錄可用以下格式，每輪複製一次。

```text
AccessSTEM AI V5 — Conversation Record
Date and time (Taipei): [actual recording time]
Participant: [team member alias]
Lesson: y = (x - 3)^2 + 2

Turn [number] · [selected learning mode] · [selected solution style]
Team member: [question actually sent]
AccessSTEM AI: [complete reply as displayed]
```

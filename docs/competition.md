# WAICY 2026 · AI Large Language Model Track

AccessSTEM AI focuses on one service for international judges to evaluate: interactive tutoring for high-school function graphs with adjustable teaching depth, hint pacing, and explicit evidence labels.

## Schedule and eligibility

The [official submission form](https://www.waicy.org/2026-submission/) closes **November 29, 2026 at 5:00 p.m. EST** (**November 30 at 06:00 in Taiwan**). Its project description limit is 200 words. It calls for one submission per team and directs teams already registered through an official local organizer to follow that organizer's process.

The [global event page](https://www.waicy.org/waicy-2026/) lists ages 6–18, individual or team entries, remote participation, finals on December 4–6, and results on December 7. The guidebook uses different age wording in places. Confirm the entrant's eligibility and assigned slot with the organizer when applicable.

## Rubric and project evidence

| Criterion | Points | Evidence |
|---|---:|---|
| Specific AI Service | 25 | One graph tutoring task, three modes with meaningfully different replies, interactive demo |
| Iterative Development | 25 | Baseline through V5, matched model calls, recorded failures, prompt excerpts, and retests |
| Motivation & Problem Statement | 10 | The learning gap between encountering a graph and understanding its axes, trends, and relationships |
| Limitations & Considerations | 10 | Model errors, absent units, teacher review, user privacy, and the limits of accessibility testing |
| Future Development & Clear Reference | 10 | Planned validation, image and speech work, and traceable sources |
| Presentation & Communication | 10 | English demo, results explained by the team, and questions answered with evidence |
| Originality & Creativity | 10 | Editable teaching preferences, staged hints, and distinctions between given, derived, and unknown facts |

Source: [Official Large Language Models rubric](https://www.waicy.org/rubrics/).

## Submission contents

Prepare one English PDF with the service, at least three meaningfully different user profiles, motivation, a shareable final-version conversation with at least ten exchanged messages, a live chatbot link, the iteration process, limitations, future work, a conclusion, and references. Use an established citation style. The guidebook gives naming and Drive sharing instructions. See the [official track pages](https://waicy-cdn.wholeren.cn/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/WAICY-2026-Guidebook-2.pdf#page=20) and the [submission checklist](waicy-submission/submission-todo.md).
